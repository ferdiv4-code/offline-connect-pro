Privacy Policy

App: Offline Connect Pro (com.offlineconnectpro.app)
Developer: ferdiv4
Effective date: 26 November 2026

Offline Connect Pro lets people on the same Wi-Fi network or phone hotspot chat, call and share files without internet. This policy explains what the app does with information.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not require an account, phone number or email address. We do not run servers that receive, store or read your messages, calls or files. We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

Your profile name, status, chats, room details and files you receive are stored locally on your phone. Messages, calls and files travel directly between phones on your local network. You can remove this data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Permissions the app uses

  • Microphone – for voice and video calls.
  • Camera – for video calls.
  • Photos and videos – so you can choose images or videos to send.
  • Network and Wi-Fi state, multicast – to find nearby rooms and connect to other phones on your local network.
  • Internet – required by Android for local network connections and for loading ads.
  • Vibration, wake lock, audio settings – for call alerts and to keep calls working properly.

Permissions are used only for these features.

4. Advertising (Unity LevelPlay and Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Unity LevelPlay and its ad partners, and may also use Google AdMob. These providers may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and information about how you interact with ads, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or turn off your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn how they use data: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Security

Local traffic is protected only by the room code and is not end-to-end encrypted. Please use the app on networks you trust and do not share sensitive information through it.

6. Children

Offline Connect Pro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date above.
